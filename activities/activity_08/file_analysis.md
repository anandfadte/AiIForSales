1. DSO figure Chen cited — the EXACT number (expect ~48 days)

5. CTO's comments about the internal build — are they still building or reconsidering?
6. Any mentions of Infosys or other competitors being evaluated?

Cross-reference with web research already in this conversation. Where does the transcript confirm or update what we found in the news? 400 words.
