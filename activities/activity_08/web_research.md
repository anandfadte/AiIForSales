# Web Research
> Find breaking news and the CFO's decision-making profile. (~4 min)

## plugin
Web Search

## context

This is a live-deal scenario. The web search is searching for things that happened in the LAST 30 DAYS — Q3 earnings, leadership changes, competitor moves. Run both searches in the **same conversation**.

## prompt
Search for QuantumEdge Technologies' developments in the last 30 days. $4.8B enterprise software (cloud ERP, supply chain), Austin TX. I need:
1. Q3 earnings results — specific numbers: DSO, margin, revenue
2. Any leadership changes — especially SVP Operations Mark Torres
3. Strategic announcements — any transformation initiatives, vendor selections
4. Competitor activity — specifically Infosys O2C case studies in enterprise software
5. Analyst sentiment — any downgrades or commentary on operational efficiency

I have a CFO meeting in 48 hours about a $6M O2C deal. I need intelligence that changes how I walk in. 600 words.

## prompt_2
Search for Sarah Chen, CFO of QuantumEdge Technologies. I know she's ex-Goldman Sachs. I need:
1. Her professional background and decision-making style
2. Any public statements about the board's 12-month mandate
3. Conference appearances — SIBOS, AFP, or other CFO forums
4. What she values in vendor relationships (from any interviews or articles)
5. Anything about her leadership approach to cost reduction and financial transformation

I have 45 minutes with her in 48 hours. What would make her take me seriously? 300 words.

## tip

The executive profile search is specifically about how she MAKES DECISIONS — not just her background. Former Goldman Sachs means she thinks in NPV, IRR, and basis points. If you can speak that language fluently, you've differentiated before the meeting starts.

## checkpoint

Do you have Q3 numbers that are more recent than what's in your CRM? And one insight about Sarah Chen's decision-making style that would change how you open the meeting? Both should come from your web research, not from memory.
