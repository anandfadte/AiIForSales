## scenario

You're a Client Partner managing **Legal & General Group** (£1.1T assets under management, UK's leading insurer and investment manager). 18 months of relationship. You have a meeting with **CFO Catherine Morris** in 48 hours about a **£5M finance operations transformation engagement**.

She said: *"Come with numbers — ROI, payback period, why invest now versus next fiscal year."*

**The complication:** Legal & General just released Q3 earnings. You haven't analysed them. Your CRM shows their expense ratio at 7.8% — but Q3 results show 8.2%, a 40bps deterioration. If you cite 7.8%, Morris will know you haven't done your homework. A competitor (Capgemini) published an insurance operations case study last week. You need LIVE intelligence, not last quarter's notes.

## context

**The board mandate:** 150bps expense ratio improvement in 18 months. This is the hook.
