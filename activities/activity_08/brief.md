# Scenario Brief
> You have 48 hours. Understand what just changed. (~3 min)

## scenario

You're a Client Partner managing **QuantumEdge Technologies** ($4.8B enterprise software, Austin TX). 18 months of relationship. You have a meeting with **CFO Sarah Chen** in 48 hours about a **$6M O2C transformation engagement**.

She said: *"Come with numbers — ROI, payback period, why invest now versus next fiscal year."*

**The complication:** QuantumEdge just released Q3 earnings. You haven't analyzed them. There are rumors of a leadership change in operations. A competitor (Infosys) published an O2C case study. You need LIVE intelligence, not last quarter's notes.

## context

Sarah Chen is ex-Goldman Sachs. She'll dismiss anything that doesn't use her own Q3 numbers. The old 45-day DSO figure in your CRM is now wrong — Q3 showed 48 days. If you cite 45, she'll know you haven't done your homework.

**The board mandate:** 200bps margin recovery in 12 months. This is the hook.

**Time: 25 minutes.**

## do_this

1. The meeting is in 48 hours. What are the THREE things that most changed since your last touchpoint — and how does each change your pitch?
2. Sarah Chen said "ROI, payback period, why now." Write the three-sentence opening that starts with HER Q3 numbers, not Genpact's capabilities.
3. How do you handle the sponsor risk (SVP Mark Torres may be leaving)? Write one sentence that acknowledges the risk without making it the conversation.

## checkpoint

Can your team articulate why the Q3 earnings release changes how you should walk into this meeting? If you're planning to pitch the same way you would have 2 weeks ago, you haven't used the intelligence stack.
