# Polish
> Polish for a CFO who wants numbers, not slides. Run /human last, always. (~4 min)

## context

Catherine Morris is ex-Goldman Sachs. She reads financial statements for breakfast. /human transforms the compound intelligence into the precise, confident language she'll take seriously — no filler, every sentence earns its place.

## prompt
/human Create a deal intelligence dossier for my CFO meeting. Catherine Morris, ex-Goldman Sachs, data-driven. Q3 shows 8.2% expense ratio (40bps deterioration from 7.8%) and 150bps board mandate in 18 months. Capgemini just published an insurance operations case study. Polish into Goldman-grade language:

1. 60-SECOND OPENING — references HER Q3 numbers and board mandate (not Genpact capabilities)
2. ROI MODEL — in her language (IRR, NPV, payback period, basis points, expense ratio)
3. 'WHY NOW' — cost-of-waiting quantification (each month of delay = £X in expense drift)
4. CAPGEMINI COUNTER-POSITIONING — what they're claiming and why Genpact is different for UK insurance
5. 3 QUESTIONS TO ASK HER — shows we're strategic, not just selling

Goldman tone: precise, confident, no filler. Every sentence earns its place. 800 words.

## reveal_good

- Dossier uses Q3 data — 8.2% expense ratio, 150bps mandate, 18-month timeline — not the old CRM 7.8%
- Catherine Morris' exact quotes woven in — board mandate language, expense deterioration reference
- Capgemini competitive threat countered — what they're claiming and why Genpact is different for L&G
- ROI model uses HER numbers, not benchmarks — L&G-specific expense ratio improvement in £M
- /human output is Goldman-grade — Morris would take this seriously
- Web research added breaking context that deal history alone didn't have
