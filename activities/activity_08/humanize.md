# Humanize
> Polish for a CFO who wants numbers, not slides. Run /human last, always. (~4 min)

## plugin
File Analysis

## context

Sarah Chen is ex-Goldman Sachs. She reads financial statements for breakfast. /human transforms the compound intelligence into the precise, confident language she'll take seriously — no filler, every sentence earns its place.

## prompt
/human Create a deal intelligence dossier for my CFO meeting. Sarah Chen, ex-Goldman Sachs, data-driven. Q3 shows 48-day DSO and 200bps margin compression. Board mandate: 12 months to recovery. Polish into Goldman-grade language:

1. 60-SECOND OPENING — references HER Q3 numbers and board mandate (not Genpact capabilities)
2. ROI MODEL — in her language (IRR, NPV, payback period, basis points)
3. 'WHY NOW' — cost-of-waiting quantification (each month of delay = $X)
4. SPONSOR RISK TALKING POINT — Mark Torres contingency in one confident sentence
5. INFOSYS COUNTER-POSITIONING — what they're claiming and why Genpact is different
6. 3 QUESTIONS TO ASK HER — shows we're strategic, not just selling

Goldman tone: precise, confident, no filler. Every sentence earns its place. 800 words.

## reveal_good

- Dossier uses Q3 data — DSO 48, margin 16.8%, $32M locked — not the old 45-day numbers
- Sarah Chen's exact quotes woven in — 'accelerating partner evaluation,' board's 12-month mandate
- Sponsor risk addressed — contingency plan if Mark Torres leaves
- Infosys competitive threat countered — what they're claiming and why Genpact is different
- ROI model uses HER numbers, not benchmarks — '$32M working capital release at 48→30 DSO'
- /human output is Goldman-grade — Sarah Chen would take this seriously
- Web research added breaking context that deal history alone didn't have

## reveal_takeaway

Sarah Chen will know in 60 seconds whether you did your homework. The test: does your opening reference her Q3 numbers (48-day DSO, 200bps compression, 12-month board mandate) within the first 3 sentences? If it starts with 'Genpact is a leading...' you've already lost her. The compound intelligence stack is what lets you open with HER numbers, not yours.
