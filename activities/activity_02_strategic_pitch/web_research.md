# Web Research
> Find Cascade's real financial position and industry pressures. (~4 min)

## plugin
Web Search

## context

Run both searches in the **same Claude conversation**. Financial intelligence first, then industry urgency. Torres will know if you used a generic industry stat where you could have used Cascade's actual number.

## prompt
Search the web for Cascade Retail Holdings' latest financial performance. They're a $3.9B omnichannel retailer in Seattle. I need:
1. Latest quarterly earnings — revenue, margins, e-commerce growth
2. Analyst sentiment — are analysts bullish or cautious? Any downgrades?
3. Peer comparison — how does their 4.2% operating margin compare to Target, Nordstrom, Macy's, Kohl's?
4. Working capital performance — any mentions of DSO, cash conversion cycle, or AP/AR efficiency
5. Strategic announcements — any transformation initiatives, vendor selections, or technology investments

I'm building a CFO business case. I need THEIR numbers and how they compare. 600 words.

## prompt_2
Search for pressures facing omnichannel retailers that create urgency for operations transformation:
1. Are retail peers investing in back-office automation? Any recent case studies or announcements?
2. What's the cost of manual O2C processes for retailers at Cascade's scale?
3. Any analyst reports on retail operational efficiency as a competitive advantage?
4. How is the shift to e-commerce (now 28% for Cascade, growing 18% YoY) straining traditional back-office processes?
5. Any regulatory or accounting changes affecting retail O2C processes?

Connect to urgency — why can't the CFO wait until next fiscal year? 400 words.

## tip

The "why now" argument is worth more than the ROI calculation. Find a live market fact (competitor announcement, analyst downgrade, regulatory deadline) that makes inaction costly — not just slower.

## checkpoint

Do you have at least one live fact about Cascade's competitive position that isn't from their website? And one trend that creates urgency for a retail CFO to act NOW rather than next fiscal year?
