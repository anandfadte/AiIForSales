# Scenario Brief
> Understand what this CFO needs before you build a single slide. (~3 min)

## scenario

You're a Client Partner at Genpact pursuing a **$9M engagement** with Cascade Retail Holdings — a $3.9B omnichannel retailer headquartered in Seattle.

Their CFO, **Rebecca Torres**, agreed to one meeting: *"Bring a business case that speaks CFO, not consultant. I want ROI, payback period, and why this can't wait. If I don't see numbers from OUR business — not generic benchmarks — I'm done in 15 minutes."*

Current state: 44-day DSO, 6.8% invoice error rate, $28M locked in working capital. They process 1.8M invoices/year across 6,200 suppliers.

## context

Torres is former Nordstrom VP Finance — she reads financial statements for breakfast. Generic consulting language ("unlock value", "synergies", "transformation journey") will end the meeting. The business case must use HER numbers, not industry benchmarks.

**Time: 25 minutes.**

## do_this

1. As a pod, identify: what is the ONE financial argument that would make a retail CFO lean forward?
2. What's the "why now" — why can't Torres wait until next fiscal year?
3. List the three numbers from the scenario that you MUST include in the business case. What would it mean for Cascade if each improved by 20%?

## checkpoint

Can your team articulate why Rebecca Torres specifically would care — using her background (Nordstrom VP Finance), her mandate (150bps margin improvement in 18 months), and her stated requirement (ROI in year one)? Generic business cases fail her test.
