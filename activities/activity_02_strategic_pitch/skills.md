# Skills Chain
> Run /cxo-strategic-brief then /cfo-deck-builder in the same conversation. (~7 min)

## skill_chain
/cxo-strategic-brief → /cfo-deck-builder → /human

## context

The skill sequence matters. /cxo-strategic-brief frames the narrative first. /cfo-deck-builder builds the financial model on top of that strategic frame. Run them in order in the **same conversation**.

## prompt
/cxo-strategic-brief Prepare a C-level strategic framing for CFO Rebecca Torres at Cascade Retail Holdings for a $9M O2C transformation engagement. Torres is former Nordstrom VP Finance — data-driven, hates fluff. Use all research in this conversation: web financials, investor presentation analysis (she said margin compression is 'entirely operational'), and our deal context. Frame Genpact as the partner who delivers operational margin recovery — not just process improvement but measurable financial return. 600 words.

## prompt_2
/cfo-deck-builder Build a CFO business case for Rebecca Torres at Cascade Retail Holdings. $9M engagement over 3 years.

Use HER numbers (from investor presentation + web research):
- DSO: 44 days → target 30 days (peer average 35)
- Invoice error rate: 6.8% → target 2%
- O2C cost: $5.20/invoice → target $3.00
- Working capital locked: $28M excess
- Her 150bps margin improvement mandate in 18 months

She said: 'I will not sign a deal that doesn't show me ROI in year one.'

Build: ROI model (NPV, IRR), payback period (monthly cash flow), 'why now' (cost of waiting), downside case, 3 opening statements for the meeting. 800 words.

## tip

Notice how /cfo-deck-builder produces a sharper ROI model because /cxo-strategic-brief already established the strategic frame? Each skill compounds the previous. The financial argument is stronger when it sits inside a strategic narrative.

## checkpoint

Do you have a year-one ROI figure that uses Cascade's own numbers? And a "cost of waiting" calculation — what does each month of delay cost Torres in working capital? If either uses a benchmark rather than Cascade's specific data, go back and extract the actual number.
