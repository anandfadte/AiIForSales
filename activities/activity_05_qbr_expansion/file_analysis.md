# File Analysis
> Extract CEO Santos' exact words from the earnings transcript. (~4 min)

## plugin
File Analysis

## context

The earnings transcript is your most powerful source for this activity. Santos' exact words about each expansion area become your opening argument. Upload it NOW, before running the skills.

Upload **earnings_transcript_excerpt.docx** using the File Analysis plugin, then run the prompt below in the same conversation.

## prompt
I'm uploading Horizon Energy Solutions' Q3 earnings call transcript. Analyze:
1. CEO Santos' 3 operational priorities — how do they map to our expansion pitch?
2. CFO Kim's comments about replicating the F&A model — this is our opening
3. The analyst question about IT ops — Santos' answer about wanting 'domain expertise, not a generalist'
4. Supply chain comment — 'embarrassingly manual' — what does this signal?
5. Any competitive intelligence — who are they evaluating?
6. Urgency signals — what language suggests they're ready to move?

Cross-reference with web research. 400 words.

## tip

"Embarrassingly manual" is a gift. If Santos said it about supply chain, she's already sold on the problem — your job is just to show her the solution. Use her exact words back to her: "You described supply chain as embarrassingly manual in Q3. Here's what a modern supply chain looks like."

## checkpoint

Do you have a direct CEO quote for each of the 3 expansion areas? The expansion pitch is 10x stronger when you can say "as you noted in your Q3 earnings call..." than when you're asserting her priorities without evidence.
