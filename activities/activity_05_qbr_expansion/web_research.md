# Web Research
> Find Horizon Energy's latest position and market trends for each expansion area. (~4 min)

## plugin
Web Search

## context

Run both searches in the **same conversation**. The first builds account intelligence. The second builds market urgency for each of the 3 expansion areas. Santos will know if your expansion pitch is based on last year's intelligence.

## prompt
Search for Horizon Energy Solutions' latest performance and strategic direction. $7.3B utility across 6 US states. I need:
1. Latest earnings — revenue, margins, operational highlights
2. CEO Maria Santos' stated priorities — especially around IT modernization, customer experience, supply chain
3. Renewable energy transition progress — 28% of generation capacity
4. Any mentions of vendor evaluation or outsourcing strategy changes
5. Industry positioning — how do they compare to Duke Energy, Exelon, Southern Company?
6. Any regulatory developments affecting their operations

I'm preparing for a QBR where I'll pitch expansion into IT ops, customer service, and supply chain. 600 words.

## prompt_2
Search for trends in the utility/energy industry that support outsourcing or transformation in these 3 areas:
1. IT Operations — cybersecurity threats to utilities, grid modernization IT needs, NERC CIP compliance
2. Customer Service — utility customer experience trends, AI chatbot adoption, digital service channels, J.D. Power benchmarks
3. Supply Chain — utility procurement modernization, $2B+ spend optimization, sustainability in supply chain

For each: market size, peer investments, and why NOW is the right time for a $7B utility to act. 500 words.

## tip

The expansion pitch fails if it looks like it was prepared before this QBR. Live intelligence (what happened at Horizon in the last quarter) makes the expansion feel timely, not opportunistic.

## checkpoint

For each of the 3 expansion areas: can you name one live market fact that makes NOW the right time for Santos to invest? Not a Genpact capability — a market reality she's already facing.
