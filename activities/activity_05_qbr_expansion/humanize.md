## plugin

## context

## prompt
/human Polish the QBR expansion section for CEO Maria Santos. She's ex-Exelon — she knows energy operations deeply. Rules:
1. STRATEGIC, NOT SALESY — frame as 'here's how we help you achieve YOUR priorities' not 'here's what we sell'
2. REFERENCE HER WORDS — 'domain expertise, not a generalist,' 'embarrassingly manual,' 'replicate the F&A model'
3. INDUSTRY LANGUAGE — speak energy: grid, SCADA, NERC CIP, J.D. Power, renewable transition
4. BRIDGE FROM PERFORMANCE — start with the F&A results ($4.1M savings, 99.5%) then pivot to 'imagine this across IT, CX, and supply chain'
5. QUANTIFY EVERYTHING — for each expansion area, estimated annual impact in $M
6. CEO TONE — not a summary of capabilities but a strategic conversation starter

Total: 800 words. Make Santos say 'tell me more about the IT ops piece.'
