# Skills Chain
> Run the 4-skill sequence in the same conversation. (~7 min)

## skill_chain
/earnings-agent → /account-research → /cxo-strategic-brief → /solution-assistant → /human

## context

This is the longest chain in the program. Run all 4 skills in the **same conversation**, in order. Each skill builds on the previous one. If you start a new conversation, you lose the compound effect.

Watch the time — with 4 skills to run before /human in Phase 6, pace yourself.

## prompt
/earnings-agent Horizon Energy Solutions — $7.3B utility. Analyze financial trajectory to frame 3 expansion opportunities (IT ops, customer service, supply chain). Focus on: revenue and margin trends, CapEx allocation, cost structure in IT/CX/procurement, peer comparison on operational efficiency, and working capital implications. I need financial framing that CEO Santos and CFO Kim will respect. 500 words.

## prompt_2
/account-research Horizon Energy Solutions — I manage an existing $8M/year F&A engagement (3 years, strong performance: 99.5% accuracy, $4.1M savings). I'm pitching expansion into IT ops, customer service, and supply chain at the QBR. The CEO is attending for the first time. Pull full account intelligence — engagement history, satisfaction data, cross-sell potential, solution fit for energy IT operations, internal references from similar utility expansions, and competitive intelligence (Infosys has their IT ops with contract expiring in 8 months). 600 words.

## tip

After running /earnings-agent and /account-research, run /cxo-strategic-brief to frame the expansion for a CEO: "Frame the 3 expansion areas as strategic responses to Santos' stated priorities — not a vendor pitch." Then run /solution-assistant to map specific capabilities. This sequential build is what produces CEO-grade output.

## checkpoint

After all 4 skills: does the output open with Horizon's priorities (from earnings transcript and web research) BEFORE mentioning any Genpact capability? Maria Santos will decide in the first 3 minutes whether this is a strategic conversation or a vendor pitch. The order is the test.
