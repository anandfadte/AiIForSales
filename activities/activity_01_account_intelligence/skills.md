# Financial Pulse
> Run /earnings-agent on Barclays. Build the financial case. (~7 min)

## skill_chain
/earnings-agent

## context

Stay in the same conversation — do NOT start a new chat. /earnings-agent builds the financial case using all the account context already in this conversation. The numbers from this phase become your opening hook with the VP of Ops.

## prompt
/earnings-agent Barclays plc — Build a financial intelligence brief for a Genpact sales meeting with their VP of Operations. This conversation already contains the full account profile from /account-research. Focus on: cost efficiency ratio trends, digital transformation investment, operational cost per transaction, efficiency programme progress, and what the latest results reveal about where operations is under pressure. What financial signal would make a VP Operations say "you understand our business"? 500 words.

## transition

Stay in the same conversation — do NOT start a new chat. Run /human in Phase 3 to combine the account profile and financial intelligence into a polished pre-meeting brief.

## tip

The best opening in the VP meeting starts with a number from their own financials — not an industry benchmark. If /earnings-agent surfaces Barclays' actual cost efficiency ratio or a specific operational metric from their last earnings call, that's your hook. "Your Q3 results showed..." lands harder than "UK banks typically..."

## checkpoint

Do you have at least one specific financial signal that speaks to operational pressure — not just revenue or profit? A VP of Operations cares about cost efficiency, processing volumes, and automation progress. If your /earnings-agent output is only about commercial performance, run a more targeted prompt focusing on operations specifically.
