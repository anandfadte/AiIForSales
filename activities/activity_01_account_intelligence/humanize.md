# Humanize
> Polish everything into executive-grade intelligence. Run /human last, always. (~4 min)

## plugin
Web Search

## context

/human is the last step in every activity. It transforms the raw compound intelligence — web research, annual report analysis, CRM data, skill outputs — into language a VP of Operations would take seriously.

Do NOT run /human before the other phases are complete. The polish is only as good as the intelligence underneath it.

## prompt
/human Take everything in this conversation — the web research, annual report analysis, CRM data, /account-research output, and /earnings-agent financial analysis — and polish it into a pre-meeting intelligence pack for my 30-minute meeting with James Morrison, VP Operations at Meridian Health Systems.

Requirements:
1. EXECUTIVE TONE — this should read like a senior healthcare advisor prepared it, not an AI
2. NO JARGON — replace consulting-speak with plain, confident language
3. DATA-BACKED — every claim supported by a specific number from our research
4. PERSONALIZED — reference James Morrison's Mayo Clinic background and his HIMSS presentation comments
5. ACTIONABLE — not just information, but what to DO with it in the meeting

Structure:
- Executive Summary (3 sentences — the 'why this meeting matters')
- Company Snapshot (current state with live data)
- Financial Case (the numbers that matter)
- 5 Opening Talking Points (what to say in the first 5 minutes)
- Pain-to-Solution Map (their problems → our capabilities)
- Recommended Meeting Agenda (how to structure the 30 minutes)
- Risk Factors (what could go wrong, how to handle it)

Total: 800 words. Every sentence must earn its place. If James Morrison read this, he should think 'this person understands healthcare operations, not just sales.'

## tip

Read the /human output aloud. If you stumble over a sentence or it sounds like it was written by a committee, it still needs work. The test: would a senior healthcare advisor be proud to put their name on this?

## reveal_good

- Intelligence pack references LIVE data — news from the last 30 days, not generic company info
- Annual report insights are specific — exact quotes from the CFO, precise metrics (52-day AR, 9.2% denial rate)
- Executive profile is personalized — references Morrison's Mayo Clinic background and HIMSS comments
- Financial case uses THEIR numbers, not industry averages — '$8.7M freed per day of AR reduction'
- /human polish is evident — executive tone, no AI-speak, reads like a senior advisor wrote it
- All 4 intelligence layers are present and tagged — [WEB] [DOC] [FOLDER] [SKILL]
- Meeting agenda is specific — not 'discuss opportunities' but 'reference AR days in minute 3, pivot to Genpact Cora demo'

## reveal_takeaway

Ask yourself: if James Morrison read this pack, would he take the meeting seriously or think 'another vendor who Googled us'? The difference is in the layers — web research alone gives you Google. Add the annual report and you have context. Add skills and you have Genpact-specific intelligence. Add /human and you have executive credibility. All four layers together is what makes the first 5 minutes count.
