# Web Research
> Search for live signals about Meridian Health and VP James Morrison. (~4 min)

## plugin
Web Search

## context

Run both searches in the **same Claude conversation** — this is where your intelligence compound starts building. Use the Web Search plugin (enable it in Claude if not already on).

## do_this

Run both prompts below in the same Claude conversation, one after the other.

## prompt
Search the web for Meridian Health Systems' latest developments. They're a $3.2B regional hospital network in Minneapolis, MN with 12 hospitals and 45 clinics. I have a first meeting with their VP of Operations in 5 days.

I need:
1. Recent news — acquisitions, partnerships, executive hires, strategic announcements (last 6 months)
2. Industry context — what challenges are regional hospital systems facing right now? Margin pressure, labor shortages, regulatory changes?
3. Competitive landscape — who are their operational partners? Any recent vendor changes?
4. Reputation — how are they perceived by patients, employees, and the industry?
5. Growth trajectory — are they acquiring, expanding services, entering new markets?

Focus on intelligence I can reference in a meeting with a VP of Operations who values domain expertise. 600 words max.

## prompt_2
Now search for James Morrison, VP of Operations at Meridian Health Systems. I need to personalize my pitch for a 30-minute meeting. He warned me: "Impress me in the first 5 minutes."

Find:
1. Professional background — previous companies and roles (I know he was at Mayo Clinic)
2. Public statements — conference talks at HIMSS, HFMA, or other healthcare events
3. Published articles or interviews about operations, healthcare efficiency, or transformation
4. Leadership style — what does he value? What frustrates him?
5. Any personal interests or affiliations that might help build rapport

I need 3 specific things I can reference in the first 5 minutes that show I researched HIM, not just his company. 400 words max.

## tip

Tag every web finding with [WEB] so you can trace it later. Specific searches outperform vague ones: "Meridian Health Systems Minneapolis Q3 2024 operational challenges" beats "Meridian Health news."

## checkpoint

After both searches: can you name one live fact about Meridian (not from their website) and one specific thing about James Morrison that would personalise the first 5 minutes? If not, refine your search terms and go again.
