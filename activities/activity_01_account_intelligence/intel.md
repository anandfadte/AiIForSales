## context

/account-research pulls Genpact's institutional intelligence on this account — existing engagement history, sector fit, similar client references, and competitive intelligence tailored to UK banking. Run it in the same conversation you'll use for Phase 3.

Build a full account profile: Barclays' strategic priorities, operational efficiency programme details, digital transformation initiatives, relevant Genpact engagement history in UK financial services, sector fit, and references from similar UK banking accounts. 600 words.

## tip

## checkpoint
