# Live Intel

> Search for what has happened at this account in the last 90 days. (~5 minutes)

## research_mode
Web Search Plugin | Surfacing live market signals that your CRM data is already too old to show

## context

Web search gives you what happened recently. Earnings calls, press releases, LinkedIn posts from executives, news coverage — these are the signals most account teams miss because they rely on CRM data that is already six months stale. The VP will notice if your view of their world is out of date. One live signal beats ten generic facts.

## tools_this_phase
Web Search (Claude plugin) — must be enabled before this phase.

## do_this

1. Use Web Search to find Meridian Health news from the last 90 days: earnings, executive announcements, operational changes, partnerships, acquisitions. Tag each finding [WEB].
2. Search for the VP of Operations by name: recent LinkedIn activity, speaking engagements, any public statements about operational priorities.
3. Search for the top operational pressures facing US healthcare networks this quarter. What challenges are showing up repeatedly across the sector? Tag these [WEB].
4. For each finding, write one sentence: what does this mean for the meeting?

## prompting_guideline

Use Web Search to run focused searches, then synthesise in Claude. Do not ask Claude to guess at search results — actually run the searches first, then ask Claude to interpret them. Try: "Using Web Search, find the last three significant announcements from Meridian Health. For each one, tell me what it implies about the VP of Operations' current priorities and what it suggests I should ask or avoid in the meeting."

## checkpoint

Do you have at least two findings that were not in your CRM? If everything you found matches what your account team already knew, your searches were not specific enough. Try searching for the VP by name, not just the company.
