# File Analysis
> Upload the annual report and extract the numbers that matter. (~4 min)

## plugin
File Analysis

## context

Upload the annual report excerpt from your folder data. This is where you move from public news to what management actually said in their own filings. The best intelligence is hiding in the CFO's footnotes, not the CEO's summary.

## do_this

Upload **annual_report_excerpt.docx** using the File Analysis plugin (paperclip icon in Claude), then run the prompt below in the **same conversation**.

## prompt
I'm uploading Meridian Health Systems' Annual Report FY2024 excerpt. Analyze this document and extract:

1. TOP OPERATIONAL PRIORITIES — what did the CEO and management say matters most for FY2025?
2. PAIN POINTS — which financial metrics indicate operational challenges? (AR days, denial rates, costs per claim, margin compression)
3. INVESTMENT PLANS — where are they spending money? How much on technology and operations?
4. MANAGEMENT QUOTES — exact quotes from the CFO and VP Operations about operational needs and vendor expectations
5. ACQUISITION INTEGRATION — what challenges does integrating 2 new hospitals create for operations?
6. VENDOR SIGNALS — any mentions of evaluating external partners, outsourcing, or seeking domain expertise?

Cross-reference with the web research already in this conversation. Where do their public filings confirm or contradict what's in the news? 500 words max.

## tip

Say "Analyze this document:" before uploading — it primes Claude to process the file analytically rather than summarise it generically. The best extractions come from asking for SPECIFIC metrics (AR days, denial rates) not "key highlights."

## checkpoint

Do you have at least 3 specific numbers from the annual report (e.g., 52-day AR, 9.2% denial rate, $12.80/claim cost)? Numbers from THEIR filings beat industry benchmarks every time. James Morrison will know if you used a benchmark where you could have used his own CFO's number.
