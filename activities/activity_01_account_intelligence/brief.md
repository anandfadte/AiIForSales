# Scenario Brief
> Read the scenario. Name the pressure. Download your files. (~3 min)

## scenario

You are the Account Head at Genpact pursuing a new logo in financial services. Your Sales Director has arranged a first meeting with the **VP of Operations at Barclays UK** — overseeing retail banking operations across 25 million+ customers. The meeting is in **5 days**.

The VP, **Alex Thornton**, agreed to a 30-minute meeting and said: *"I take a lot of these calls. Show me you understand Barclays' operations — not just what your company sells. We're in the middle of a significant efficiency programme. If you can't connect to that, we're done in 10 minutes."*

## context

Your deliverable: a pre-meeting intelligence pack — Barclays overview, executive profile, financial signals, 5 talking points for the first 5 minutes, operational pain points mapped to Genpact capabilities, and a recommended meeting agenda polished for executive tone.

**Time: 25 minutes.**

## do_this

**Step 1 — Read the scenario above** until you can say what Thornton's operational pressure is in one sentence.

**Step 2 — Name the pressure.** As a pod, complete this sentence out loud: *"Thornton is under pressure to ______ because ______."* You'll use this in Phase 2.

**Step 3 — Download your activity files** from the panel on this page. They give you background context on the account.

## checkpoint

Can your pod complete the sentence — "Thornton is under pressure to ___ because ___" — without searching anything yet? If not, re-read the scenario. The answer is in it.
