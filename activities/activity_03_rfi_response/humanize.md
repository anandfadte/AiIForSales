## context

Direct Line's evaluators will score your responses. /human transforms technically correct answers into confident, specific, submission-ready text that scores higher. Remove all hedge language before submitting.

## prompt
/human Polish all 15 RFI responses for client-ready submission to Direct Line Group. Rules:
- Direct Line said 'generic responses will be scored lower' — every answer must reference something specific about their business or our web research

- Evidence-backed: every claim supported by a number, a client reference, or a case study
- Consistent format: each answer starts with a direct response, then evidence, then differentiator
- Remove AI-speak: no 'leveraging,' 'holistic,' 'cutting-edge,' 'state-of-the-art'
- Read each answer aloud — does it sound like a confident expert or a cautious committee?

Total polish: all 15 answers, maintaining specificity from the plugin outputs while adding executive confidence.
