## plugin

## context

TrueNorth's evaluators will score your responses. /human transforms technically correct answers into confident, specific, submission-ready text that scores higher. Remove all hedge language before submitting.

## prompt
/human Polish all 15 RFI responses for client-ready submission to TrueNorth Insurance Group. Rules:
- TrueNorth said 'generic responses will be scored lower' — every answer must reference something specific about their business or our web research
- Confident tone: 'We deliver' not 'We believe we can deliver'
- Evidence-backed: every claim supported by a number, a client reference, or a case study
- Consistent format: each answer starts with a direct response, then evidence, then differentiator
- Remove AI-speak: no 'leveraging,' 'holistic,' 'cutting-edge,' 'state-of-the-art'
- Read each answer aloud — does it sound like a confident expert or a cautious committee?

Total polish: all 15 answers, maintaining specificity from the skill outputs while adding executive confidence.
