# Web Research
> Build client intelligence and competitive context before drafting a word. (~4 min)

## plugin
Web Search

## context

TrueNorth said "generic responses will be scored lower." Your web research is what makes your answers specific. Run both searches in the **same conversation** before uploading the RFI.

## prompt
Search for TrueNorth Insurance Group — an $8.4B property & casualty insurer. I'm responding to their RFI for claims processing transformation. I need:
1. Their business overview — lines of business, geographic footprint, recent performance
2. Strategic priorities — what is their CEO/COO saying about claims modernization?
3. Technology stack — do they use Guidewire, Duck Creek, or another claims system?
4. Recent news — any leadership changes, M&A, regulatory issues, catastrophe impact?
5. Vendor relationships — any existing outsourcing partners being replaced?

I need to make my RFI responses speak THEIR language, not generic insurance language. 500 words.

## prompt_2
Search for how Accenture, Cognizant, and EXL position their insurance claims processing capabilities:
1. Accenture: their SynOps platform, insurance practice claims capabilities, recent wins
2. Cognizant: their TriZetto/claims offerings, recent insurance wins, any quality concerns
3. EXL: their analytics-led claims approach, insurance focus, how they differentiate

What will TrueNorth ALREADY be hearing from these competitors? Where can Genpact differentiate? 400 words.

## tip

The competitive search is as important as the client search. Knowing what Accenture claims lets you write answers that implicitly differentiate without naming them. If Accenture leads with SynOps, lead with specific insurance outcomes (94% fraud detection accuracy) rather than platform names.

## checkpoint

Can you name: (1) one thing specific about TrueNorth's business that should appear in your RFI answers, and (2) one claim Accenture is making that you can differentiate against? Both are needed before uploading the RFI document.
