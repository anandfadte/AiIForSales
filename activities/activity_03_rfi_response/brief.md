# Scenario Brief
> Read the RFI challenge. Name the differentiator. Download your files. (~3 min)

## scenario

TrueNorth Insurance Group — an $8.4B property & casualty insurer — issued an RFI for **claims processing transformation**. You're a Solution Architect on the bid team. The deadline is **48 hours**. Your bid team is stretched across 3 other responses. You need to produce a high-quality first draft fast.

**15 questions** covering: company overview, claims processing capabilities, AI/ML approach, technology platform, transition methodology, pricing model, and references.

The RFI explicitly states: *"We value specificity. Generic responses will be scored lower than responses that demonstrate understanding of our business."*

**Competing against:** Accenture, Cognizant, and EXL.

## context

TrueNorth's procurement team has said they're looking for "a partner with deep insurance domain expertise and proven AI capabilities — not just a technology vendor." This is the signal: technology alone won't win. Domain expertise + proven AI is the differentiation.

**Time: 25 minutes.**

## do_this

**Step 1 — Read the scenario above.** The RFI says "generic responses will be scored lower." Your brief job: agree what "generic" looks like so you can avoid it.

**Step 2 — Name the differentiator.** As a pod, complete this sentence: *"One thing Accenture CANNOT honestly say about Genpact is ____."* This becomes the spine of every differentiating answer.

## checkpoint

Can your pod complete the sentence — "One thing Accenture cannot say is ____" — without searching anything? If your answer is vague ("we have better relationships"), go sharper. The RFI is asking for proof, not positioning.
